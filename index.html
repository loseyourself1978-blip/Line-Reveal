New Game Modes

1. Wallpaper Creator Mode
Design and generate high-quality mobile wallpapers using your dressed-up anime characters. Save wallpapers directly to your device with one click.

How To Play

1. Select your favorite 3D anime beauty character

2. Choose costumes (qipao, kimono, jk, sailor, uniform) and accessories

3. Use styling tools to create unique looks

4. Take screenshots and make custom mobile wallpapers

5. Complete challenges to unlock more outfits and items

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: How to unlock more costumes?
A: Complete daily tasks, level up, and finish styling challenges to unlock qipao, kimono, jk uniform and more costumes.

Q: How to save wallpapers to my phone?
A: After styling your character, tap the "Save Wallpaper" button to store the image in your photo album.

Q: Is this game suitable for all ages?
A: Yes! The game contains family-friendly content, no inappropriate material, and is safe for all audiences.

Q: The game crashes or freezes, what should I do?
A: Restart the app or update your device system. Contact us if the problem continues.

Q: Can I play offline?
A: Yes, core dress up and styling features work without an internet connection.
